ZOEY'S STORE 22 — VIOLET DUSK E-COMMERCE FRONT END

Brand: Zoey's Store | Logo credit: OCC Technology
Palette: #502D55 (violet), #935073 (rose), #F6DBC0 (peach), #F8F4E9 (cream), black and white. No green theme colours are used.

PAGES
- index.html: home and hero slider
- shop.html: product collection
- product.html: product details and four colour choices
- cart.html: quantities, remove item, totals
- checkout.html: delivery/customer details
- payment.html: bank transfer, USDT, or cash selection
- payment-instructions.html: bank/USDT instructions placeholder
- receipt.html: order summary and print receipt
- about.html, contact.html, faq.html, delivery.html, privacy.html, terms.html

ASSETS
- assets/css/style.css: single shared stylesheet
- assets/js/store.js: shared localStorage and page interactions
- assets/img/occ-logo.svg: OCC logo

RUN
Extract the folder and open index.html with VS Code Live Server. Internet is required for Google Fonts, Font Awesome, sample Unsplash images, and embedded Google Maps.

IMPORTANT
This is a front-end demo. localStorage is browser-only and is not a secure order database. Bank transfer and USDT details must be replaced with verified business details. Cash, bank transfer, and USDT selections do not verify payment or move funds. Add a secure server-side payment/order flow before accepting live orders. The WhatsApp buttons open WhatsApp with a prepared message but do not target a specific business phone number yet; replace the wa.me URL with your official number when available. The map points to Abakaliki, Ebonyi State, Nigeria.
